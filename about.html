Purpose of this forum

This site is intended to build a factual community record of outdoor-lighting conditions across Bosque County, with particular attention to residents' observations concerning the Lhoist Clifton Lime Plant and broader county-wide light pollution.

The forum does not assume that every reported light originates from Lhoist, that every impact is unlawful, or that every submitted statement is correct. Published reports are community submissions, not official findings.

What makes a strong report?

Record the date and approximate time, your general observation area, the direction you were looking, cloud cover or weather, the apparent source if known, and how long the condition lasted. Repeated photos from the same location and camera settings can be particularly useful for showing changes over time.

Public records & background

Lhoist's Clifton Lime Plant is a regulated facility in Bosque County. Official TCEQ permitting documents and records can be used to verify facility and permit information. Texas law also contains specific provisions governing when counties may regulate outdoor lighting; the existence and scope of any locally applicable requirement should be verified before claiming a violation.

  • TCEQ — Lhoist Clifton Lime Plant air permit
  • TCEQ Records Online
  • Texas Local Government Code, Chapter 240

Moderation standard

Moderators should publish specific firsthand observations and verifiable records, while rejecting threats, personal attacks, unsupported accusations of criminal or unlawful conduct, private identifying information, and duplicate/spam submissions. When feasible, corrections should be appended visibly rather than silently rewriting the historical record.
