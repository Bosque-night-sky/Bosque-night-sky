Publication rules

Reports must focus on observable conditions or documented public matters. No threats, harassment, doxxing, private personal information, impersonation, or knowingly false statements. Do not state that a person or company violated a law unless you can point to a reliable official finding. Opinions should be identified as opinions; allegations should be identified as allegations.
